THE FINDING

₹30,000/month is a target.
It is not a verified floor.

We found no evidence that one ordinary India household can reliably make that amount from set-once apps. Bandwidth income depends on purchased traffic; research panels depend on admission; compute depends on hardware and assigned jobs. The linked provider evidence appears with each app below.

Best first experiment: one documented bandwidth app on an already-running device. Investigate Salad only if you already own suitable hardware. Budget unverified rewards, drawings and speculative points at ₹0.

03 / THE FIRST WEEK

Prove a small setup before expanding.

Proposed schedule: 17–23 November 2026.
Human sign-in and provider consent required.

  1. DAY 01 · NOV 17

    Inventory and choose

    Confirm actual country, Windows version, exact GPU/RAM, real phones, authorized broadband connections, data cap, electricity tariff and available payout methods. Check whether the ISP permits sharing. Choose one bandwidth pilot; keep GPU compute conditional on existing hardware.

    Output: eligible shortlist and cost baseline.
  2. DAY 02 · NOV 18

    Check redemption, then install

    Sign in locally. Record current rate, withdrawal minimum, supported India destination, fees, device rules and permissions. Verify the official installer publisher. Start one client on one authorized connection.

    Output: one running candidate with documented terms.
  3. DAY 03 · NOV 19

    Measure a full day

    Log credited earnings, actual traffic and extra power. Check connection quality and account health. If a suitable GPU already exists, start a separate compute trial and record job hours and power; no new hardware purchase.

    Output: measured gross and cost, with idle time included.
  4. DAY 04 · NOV 20

    Check mobile research invitations

    Use real phones to inspect MobileXpression and Viewpoints eligibility. Accept data collection only after reading consent. Invitations, program completion and surveys stay manual. Do not install incompatible measurement/VPN services together.

    Output: accepted offers, or ₹0 if no eligible offer.
  5. DAY 05 · NOV 21

    Check a second candidate carefully

    If the first app is unproductive, compare a second eligible app in a separate observation window. If later running together is permitted, measure combined throughput and net income instead of adding two advertised estimates.

    Output: evidence for keeping or dropping each candidate.
  6. DAY 06 · NOV 22

    Request a payout if eligible

    Withdraw only after both threshold and time requirements are met. If not, estimate days to the threshold from measured credits; record “not yet withdrawable.” Set startup/schedules only for a pilot worth continuing.

    Output: withdrawal request or realistic threshold timeline.
  7. DAY 07 · NOV 23

    Decide using net results

    Compare reward value, actual cash received, electricity, data cost and disruption. Remove loss-making or unacceptable clients. Project the remaining November dates conservatively; separately report December's possible 30-day run rate.

    Output: continue / stop decision and a private monthly ledger.

04 / WINDOWS & AUTOMATION

Automate upkeep. Verify the income.

Start the three-Android / separate-SIM pilot ?

What can run in the background

  • Official Windows clients, using their own startup and idle-work settings.
  • A Task Scheduler reminder to record weekly credits, costs and payout status.
  • Local CSV calculations and reminders when a measured balance may reach its minimum.
  • Allowed compute schedules, with cooling and power consumption monitored.

Use normal app controls first. No earnings client or scheduled task has been installed by this research project.

What we need for your setup

  • Actual operating country and Windows version.
  • GPU model + VRAM, RAM, and spare disk capacity.
  • Number of real phones and authorized internet connections; ISP, cap and speed.
  • Electricity cost and whether the PC already stays on.
  • Available payout types and willingness to accept gift cards or crypto.

Provide descriptions, not secrets. You enter passwords, OTPs, payment details and identity documents directly on the provider's own app. Never send wallet seed phrases or commit account tokens.

Windows cannot substitute for every mobile panel. Real device activity, location, participant consent and program eligibility matter. Native Windows clients cover some bandwidth/compute apps; phone research may still require an actual eligible Android/iOS device. The number of emulators is not an earning entitlement.

06 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

A research page, not a payout endorsement.

Three parallel research workers reviewed research panels, bandwidth apps and compute/other alternatives. Primary provider pages were checked through direct HTTPS on November 17, 2026. The web-search interface returned blank results, so source claims were rechecked directly and unsupported details removed. All sources are linked with the relevant app.

Public terms establish what a provider claims. They do not prove app security, current admission, customer demand or successful payout. No app account, withdrawal, binary audit or month-long earning run was performed. No reliable independent India earnings distribution was established. Some leads remain unverified because pages were inaccessible.

Prior names: “Confluence” in the original request could not be confidently identified as an earning app. It has not been silently replaced with another product. Meta's old Study announcement is historical context, not a current Facebook Research enrollment link.

Other lead screened: Storj's official November 6, 2026 asset-sale notice describes a Chapter 11 process affecting its storage/network assets. It was excluded from the quick-cash plan; this is a specific current concern, not a general judgment on storage rental. Official notice ↗

Unknown country support, fee, payout timing or rate is labeled unknown. No affiliate links, sign-up bonuses or speculative winnings inflate the baseline. Corrections should include the official source and its date.
